I.3

Leadership Development Process

This section in other versions2018 Manual: F4 LDP

While leadership is critical to healthy Communities, effective Organizations and and dynamic Teams, leaders are always in short supply because most Groups do not employ a systematic leadership development process.

All Lizards, because they are Effective12, employ some form of a leadership development process that is constantly adjusted to suit their unique needs. While every process reflects the purpose and character of the particular Lizard in which it is housed, every useful process has a form of the same four phases:

  • First there is some kind of Schooling13, the didactic instruction that a leader receives on the timeless fundamentals of leadership.
  • Second, there is an Apprenticeship14, where young leaders are given the chance to develop the skills they learned in the classroom under the watchful eye of someone more experienced.
  • Third comes Opportunity15, the chance for a growing leader to put what he has learned into practice under conditions where he is directly responsible for the outcome. While Schooling + Apprenticeship create a potential leader, Opportunity is the required element for the man’s skill to become kinetic, to have actual impact on the Organization, Community or Team.
  • Fourth and finally is Failure16. A Lizard uses its leadership development process to intentionally court disaster in order to fully develop its leaders, because it is from Failure that we learn the most. Success teaches us less because it does not isolate what worked the way Failure isolates what doesn’t.

It is not the purpose of the QSource to prescribe a particular leadership development process, but rather to describe the elements common to a useful process.

Notes

  1. Effectiveness is the degree to which an individual leads or a Group achieves Advantage. ↩
  2. Schooling is the provision of formal leadership instruction. ↩
  3. Apprenticeship is a relationship within which positive habit transfer takes place. ↩
  4. Opportunity is a position within a Group that provides leadership experience. ↩
  5. Failure is an undesirable outcome that increases resilience. ↩
