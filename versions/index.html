About these versions

Every page on this site says which version its text comes from. The text is reproduced exactly as it appears in that version: wording, capitalization and punctuation are unchanged. Only page layout (line breaks and page numbers) has been removed.

Mistakes are kept on purpose. Misspellings, typos and slips in the originals (for example “Ecclesiastics 4:12” or “TGalations 5:22-23”) are part of the author’s voice and are preserved exactly as written. Please do not correct them.

How the versions differ

The 2017 Handbook came first (the PDF was saved in January 2017). It opens with a StartEx introduction (QSource, Groups, Leadership Development Process, Four Quadrants), begins Quadrant 1 with a QPoint on the Q himself, and covers all four quadrants, including Group Leadership and Legacy. Each QPoint has a Statement, Scripture, Socratic questions, a Synthesis and a Spur.

The 2018 Manual (May 2018) reworks the opening into a five-part Foundation (Disruption, Language, Group, LDP, Preparedness), renames the first two quadrants Get Right and Live Right, and covers those two quadrants only. It adds red highlighting for new terms and an appendix lexicon. The 2025 web edits on f3nation.com were made from this text.

Where the same QPoint appears in more than one version, its page links to the others under This section in other versions.
