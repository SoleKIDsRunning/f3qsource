The First Quadrant: DRP

  • Q1.1QThe Q is a leader who takes responsibility for the Outcome.
  • Q1.2DRPThe Q makes the daily decision to continue accelerating
  • Q1.3KingThe Q physical trains his body
  • Q1.4QueenThe Q is in control over what he eats and drinks
  • Q1.5JesterThe Q actively resists the temptations that hinder his Acceleration
  • Q1.6MThe Q’s marriage is his most important relationship
  • Q1.7ShortiesThe Q sacrifices everything for his children
  • Q1.8Shield LockThe Q forms deep horizontal bonds with other men
  • Q1.9WhetstoneThe Q helps younger men become Leaders
  • Q1.10MammonThe Q works to sustain his family and benefit the Community
  • Q1.11PrayerThe Q talks to his Creator in order to build his Faith
  • Q1.12StudyThe Q explores the foundations of his Faith
  • Q1.13MeetingThe Q publicly manifests his Faith

The Second Quadrant: IMPACT

  • Q2.1IMPACTThe Q engages in forcible contact to strong effect
  • Q2.2InfluenceThe Q’s ignites a palpable desire for action in the hearts of other men
  • Q2.3MissionalityThe Q does mostly what he does best, for the people he loves the most
  • Q2.4Positive Habit TransferThe Q teaches other men everything that he was taught
  • Q2.5AccountabilityThe Q submits himself to Standard through Enforcement and Consequence.
  • Q2.6CorrectionThe Q advocates for Virtue
  • Q2.7TargetingThe Q initiates Missionality in the hearts of other men

The Third Quadrant: Group Leadership

  • Q3.1VirtueThe Q recognizes the individual behavior, habits and ethics that are beneficial to the Group
  • Q3.2The Virtuous LeaderThe Q is an Effective Leader who seeks a Virtuous Outcome
  • Q3.3CandorThe Q graciously tells the hard truth
  • Q3.4CommitmentThe Q demonstrates unwavering loyalty and determination to both his Mission and the well being of his Groups
  • Q3.5CompetenceThe Q ensures that he is capable of performing Mission-essential Tasks
  • Q3.6ContentmentThe Q exhibits stability of emotion engendered by his consistent pursuit of Joy over Happiness
  • Q3.7CourageThe Q sets aside his fear to turn hardship into grace
  • Q3.8VisionThe Q sees the necessary Outcomes that form the path to accomplishing an Articulated Purpose
  • Q3.9ArticulationThe Q clearly describes his Vision
  • Q3.10PersuasionThe Q Influences voluntary Movement toward an Outcome
  • Q3.11ExhortationThe Q rallies his followers to sustain Movement until the Outcome is realized
  • Q3.12Leadership FoundationThe Q builds a foundation of Leadership Skills and Characteristics through a Leadership Development Process
  • Q3.13SchoolingSchooling provides the Q formal Leadership instruction to obtain the head knowledge of what a Leader is and does
  • Q3.14ApprenticeshipThe Q transfers his head knowledge into hand knowledge through Positive Habit Transfer
  • Q3.15OpportunityThe Q advances through various Leadership positions within his Groups to obtain experience
  • Q3.16FailureThe Q increases his resilience through the adversity that arises from undesirable Outcomes

The Fourth Quadrant: Legacy

  • Q4.1LegacyThe Q is a Leader who is followed even after he is dead
  • Q4.2Ultimate Life ProblemThe Q’s Legacy begins with identifying the Problem he was uniquely born to solve
  • Q4.3PurposeThe Q’s Purpose is to solve his Ultimate Life Problem
  • Q4.4Lizard BuildingThe Q builds Effective Organizations to accomplish his Purpose
  • Q4.5Developing TeamsThe Q develops Dynamic Teams that benefit Community and enable Organizations to be Effective
  • Q4.6TrustThe Q builds reliance between Team Members
  • Q4.7EquippingThe Q initiates and reinforces Teamwork by matching Members to Mission
  • Q4.8Accountability (Team)The Q sets and maintains high Standards for his Team Members
  • Q4.9Missionality (Team)The Q ensures that Mission accomplishment is always the Team’s first priority
  • Q4.10Sua Sponte LeadershipThe Q takes Individual Initiative and fosters it in other Leaders
